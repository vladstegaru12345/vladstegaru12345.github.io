Contact us

  • Email stegaru.vlad@gmail.com
  • In the app You → Help & guides → Contact us

We answer in English or Polish. We reply to complaints within 14 days, to privacy requests within one month, and we review reports within 24 hours.

Complaints and appeals

If Together does not work as our Terms of Use describe, or you disagree with something we did, write to stegaru.vlad@gmail.com, use Contact us in the app. Please include:

  • your name;
  • the email address linked to your Together account (on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Together), so we can find your account;
  • what happened and when (a screenshot helps);
  • what you would like us to do.

We reply within 14 days of receiving your complaint. The full procedure is in the Terms of Use, section 17. To complain about content or conduct, or to appeal a decision about your content or account (also without an account), see Complaints and appeals.

Report a Moment or a person

  • A Moment: open the Moment, open its options, tap Report and choose a reason (Inappropriate, Harassment or Something else).
  • A person: open the person in the Together tab and tap Report {name}, next to Disconnect. Reporting a person covers everything they sent you: Care messages, replies, their About text and goal names. You can also report someone after you have disconnected.

A person reviews every report within 24 hours. We may remove a Moment for everyone, end the connection or ban the account. We never tell the person who reported them. Together has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users; see the Terms of Use, section 8.

Intimate images shared without consent

Anyone, with or without a Together account, can ask us in writing to remove an intimate image or video of them that was shared in Together without their consent. When the request is valid, we remove the image within 48 hours and make reasonable efforts to remove known identical copies. How to send a removal request.

Disconnect (block) someone

Open the person and tap Disconnect {name}. Disconnecting blocks them from sharing with you or contacting you in Together, and stops everything you share with them, at once: Moments, Care, Pick-up and shared progress. You can reconnect only if one of you sends a new invitation and the other accepts it.

Delete your account

  1. In Together, open You → Privacy & account → Delete account.
  2. Confirm, then confirm with Apple.
  3. You are signed out right away. Deleting your account usually takes a few minutes; if anything delays it, we finish within 30 days. The screen confirms when deletion is complete, or the next time you open it.

Lost your iPhone? Sign in to Together on another iPhone with the same Apple Account and delete there, or email us from the address linked to your account. What is deleted and what remains is explained in the Privacy Policy, section 10. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

Ask for your data

To get a copy of your data, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it, email stegaru.vlad@gmail.com, ideally from the address linked to your account. We answer within one month, free of charge. Your rights and how to withdraw consent (for example to stop sharing your step total in a shared goal) are in the Privacy Policy, section 11.

Report illegal content without an account

Anyone can tell us about illegal content in Together, with or without an account. Write to stegaru.vlad@gmail.com with where the content is (for example who sent it and when), why you believe it is illegal, your name and email address (you may leave these out if you are reporting child sexual abuse material), and a statement that your notice is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

This address is also our single point of contact under the EU Digital Services Act, for users and for authorities. Languages: Polish and English.

Invitations

Together invites by code. The person inviting you opens Together → People → “Invite someone” and shows you a QR code or sends you the invitation code. On your iPhone, install Together, then tap “Have a code? Enter it” and paste the code, or tap “Scan QR code” and point the camera at theirs. You always see who invited you before you send a request, and you are connected only when they accept. An invitation cannot be used after 7 days.
