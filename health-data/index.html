1. Who this policy is for

This policy is for people in the United States, in particular Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, whose laws give "consumer health data" special protection. It is provided by VLĂDUŢ STEGARU Consultancy (section 9), which has no affiliates. Our general Privacy Policy covers all other personal data.

2. What we collect and why

CategoryWhat it isWhy we collect it and how we use it
Daily step totals (shared step goals only)Only while you choose "Share my progress with the group" in a shared step goal and confirm "I consent": your daily step total from Apple Health for today and the two days before (in a weekly goal, also the earlier days of that week), each with its day, your time zone and when it was saved.To show your progress to the people who joined that goal. Deleted when you switch sharing off, leave the goal, the goal is cancelled, the connection you joined it through ends, or you delete your account.

Kept while you share and for 12 months after your last change; when you delete your account it is kept without your identity until then.
Location during a pick-up, if a trip happens to go to or from a place of health careDuring a pick-up that the person collecting agreed to: their current position, only the latest one, never a history.To show the other person the distance and an estimated arrival time. It is deleted when sharing stops or the pick-up ends; sharing ends after 8 hours at most, and positions older than 10 minutes are deleted. We never use it to learn anything about anyone's health.
Health information you choose to writeAnything about your health you decide to include in a Care check-in, a reply, a caption, your About text or a goal name. We never ask for it.Only to deliver it to the person you chose and show it on your devices, as our Privacy Policy describes.
Kept only on your iPhoneSteps and walking distance that Together reads from Apple Health for your own goals, and your mood check-ins.To update your own goals and mood history on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not draw conclusions about your health from it.

3. Where it comes from

  • Apple Health on your iPhone, only after you allow Together to read your steps.
  • You: the choices you make in the app (for example switching step sharing on) and what you write.
  • Your iPhone's location, only during a pick-up the person collecting agreed to.

We never obtain consumer health data from other companies or data brokers.

5. How we protect it

  • We read steps only after you allow it on Apple's Health access screen, and we share step totals only after you separately tap "I consent" for that goal. Neither is part of your agreement to our Terms of Use.
  • Only the owner of the business, Vlăduţ Stegaru, can access the production systems. No employees or contractors have access.
  • Connections are encrypted, and database access is restricted row by row.
  • We do not use geofences around health care facilities, and we never use location to identify, track or contact anyone about their health.

6. Your rights and how to use them

RightWhat it meansHow to use it
AccessConfirm whether we collect or share your consumer health data, get a copy, and get a list of everyone we shared it with, with a way to contact themEmail us
DeletionHave your consumer health data deleted. Shared step totals are stored only on our servers, so deleting them there removes them from Together for everyone who could see them. Copies in backups expire within 7 daysSwitch off "Share my progress with the group" (your shared step totals are deleted at once), delete your account in the app (You → Privacy & account → Delete account), or email us
Withdrawing consentStop sharing or reading your step totals at any timeSwitch off "Share my progress with the group"; remove Together's access to Apple Health in iOS Settings; stop sharing a pick-up

How to ask: email at.together.support@gmail.com, use You → Help & guides → Contact us in the app. Someone you authorise can ask for you; we may ask for proof of that. To protect you, we check that the request comes from the account holder, usually by replying to the email address linked to your Sign in with Apple account. Requests are free, and we never treat you differently for using your rights.

When we answer: within one month. If a request is complex, we tell you within that month and answer within 45 more days at the latest.

7. Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal: reply to our answer, or email at.together.support@gmail.com with "Appeal" in the subject. We answer in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If we still decline, you can contact your state's Attorney General, for example in Washington (atg.wa.gov), Nevada (ag.nv.gov) or Connecticut (portal.ct.gov/ag).

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Before we collect or share consumer health data in a new way, we tell you in the app and ask for your consent.
