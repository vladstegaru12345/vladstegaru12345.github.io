5. The services (types and scope)

Together is a private app for you and the few people you choose. You connect only with someone who accepts your invitation, or whose invitation you accept. There is no public profile, no public feed and no way to search for strangers. We provide these services by electronic means:

  • Account: sign in with Apple, keep your profile and portrait, and delete your account.
  • Connections: invite people, accept requests, choose what you share with each person, disconnect.
  • Moments: send a photo, video or GIF, with a caption, to one person you are connected with. They can react and reply.
  • Care: send a short check-in question to someone you are connected with. They can reply, and you can acknowledge the reply.
  • Pick-up: plan a pick-up. During the trip, and only when the person collecting agrees, they share their live position.
  • Goals, shared goals and Focus: track personal goals and Focus sessions. You can join goals with a group, and you choose whether the group sees your progress.
  • Learn: practise skills and attach proofs: a photo, a video or a recording.
  • Mood: a private check-in that never leaves your iPhone.
  • Widgets and notifications: show what your people shared on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
  • Help & guides and Contact us in the app, and our website with these Terms, the Privacy Policy, support information.

Some features are not offered in every country: Pick-up is not offered on the South Korean App Store, and sharing your step total in a shared goal is not offered on the Thai, Omani, Nepali and Lebanese App Stores.

Together is free to use. Meant Together Premium is an optional paid subscription (section 19). We keep improving it, so features may change. If we remove a main feature you rely on, or make a change that clearly worsens the service for you, we will tell you in the app in advance. You can then delete your account.

9. Your safety tools

  • Report. You can report a Moment (open it, open its options, tap Report and choose a reason), or report a person ("Report {name}" on their page, next to "Disconnect {name}"). Reporting a person covers everything they sent you, including Care messages, replies, their About text and goal names. We do not tell the person who reported them.
  • Disconnect (block). "Disconnect {name}" ends your connection at once. All sharing between you stops: Moments, Care, Pick-up and shared progress. The person can no longer send you anything or contact you through Together, and can no longer see your portrait. You can only reconnect if one of you sends a new invitation, the other uses it, and the person who sent it accepts the request, so both of you have to agree. One exception: if you are both members of a shared goal that a third person created, you both remain in that goal.
  • Sensitive Content Warning (when available). Where your iPhone and Together's version support it, and you have turned on Apple's Sensitive Content Warning (or Communication Safety is on for your account), Together uses Apple's on-device analysis to blur photos and videos you receive that may contain nudity. You can choose to view them. Flagged media is not shown on your widgets. The analysis runs on your iPhone; we never see its result.
  • Word filter. Together will not send a caption, Care message, reply, About text, or goal name or unit that contains a slur, a sexual term or a threat from its list.
  • Contact us. In the app, go to You → Help & guides → Contact us, or write to at.together.support@gmail.com.

These tools reduce the risk; they cannot remove it. The blurring and the word filter will miss some content and may occasionally stop harmless content. Please report anything that slips through.
