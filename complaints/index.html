Complaints and appeals

Anyone can use this page, with or without a Together account: to complain about something in Together, or to ask us to look again at a decision we made.

An intimate image of you was shared without your consent? Use the faster route on Report an intimate image: we remove the image of a valid request within 48 hours. Someone in danger? Call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US, 999 in the UK).

1. What you can complain about

  • Content or conduct in Together that is illegal or breaks our rules, for example harassment, threats or hate. If you have the app, you can also report a Moment or a person there (open the Moment's options or the person's page, then Report); a person reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • How we handled a report you made.
  • An appeal, if we removed your content, ended one of your connections, or restricted, suspended or banned your account. Please appeal within six months of our decision.
  • Our automatic checks, if the word filter stopped something it should not have, or the Sensitive Content blur hid something it should not have.
  • How we meet our legal duties, for example under the UK Online Safety Act 2023 or the EU Digital Services Act.
  • Your personal data (see our Privacy Policy), or the service itself, if Together does not work as our Terms of Use describe.

We answer in English or Polish.

3. What to include

  1. Your name and how we can reach you (an email address or phone number). If you have an account, the email address linked to it (on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Together), so we can find it.
  2. What your complaint is about: which content, person or decision, and when it happened. Describe content rather than attaching it; never send an image of a child.
  3. Why you think it is wrong.
  4. What you would like us to do.

4. What happens next

  • We confirm that we received your complaint, usually within 2 working days.
  • A person looks at it. We never decide a complaint or an appeal by automated means alone.
  • Typical response times: reports of harmful content are reviewed within 24 hours; intimate-image removal requests are handled within 48 hours; complaints and appeals are answered within 14 days; requests about your personal data within one month.
  • We tell you what we decided and why. If an appeal succeeds, we reverse our decision where we can, for example by lifting a ban.
  • If you do not want further updates about your complaint, tell us and we will only send our final answer.
  • We do not tell the person you complained about who complained, unless the law requires it.

5. Other options

  • You can always go to court, or contact the authorities named in our Privacy Policy about your personal data.
  • If you are a consumer in Poland, free help is available from your municipal or district consumer ombudsman; see Terms of Use, section 24.
