Report an intimate image shared without consent

If an intimate image or video of you was shared in Together without your consent, you can ask us to remove it. You do not need a Together account.

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US, 999 in the UK). If someone is threatening to share an image of you, you can also contact the police.

1. How to ask us to remove an image

Send your request in writing by email to at.together.support@gmail.com with the subject "Intimate image removal request". You can make the request for yourself, or for someone else if you are authorised to act for them.

If the image was sent to you in Together, you can also report it in the app: open the Moment, open its options, tap Report and choose a reason. Please also send the email, so that we have everything this page asks for.

Please do not attach the image. Describe it instead. If it shows someone under 18, never send it to anyone; just tell us, and we will report it to the authorities.

2. What to include

  1. Your signature: a physical or electronic signature. Typing your full name at the end of the email is enough. If you act for someone else, sign with your own name and say how you are authorised to act for them.
  2. Which image, and how we can find it: a description of the image or video, who sent it (their name in Together, if you know it), who received it, and roughly when it was sent.
  3. A short statement that you believe in good faith that the image was shared without the consent of the person shown, with anything that helps us see that it was.
  4. How we can reach you: your name and an email address or phone number.

A simple email is enough, for example:

"I am asking you to remove an intimate image of me that was shared in Together without my consent. It is a photo of [description], sent by [name in Together, if known] to [recipient, if known] on or around [date]. I believe in good faith that it was shared without my consent. You can reach me at [email or phone]. Signed: [your full name]."

3. What happens next

  • We confirm that we received your request. A person reviews it within 24 hours.
  • If something we need is missing, we ask you for it straight away.
  • When the request is valid, we remove the image as soon as possible, and no later than 48 hours after we receive it. We also make reasonable efforts to find and remove any known identical copies in Together, and copies we identify as substantially the same.
  • We act against the account that shared it under our Terms of Use, which can include banning it. We report images of minors to the authorities.
  • We tell you what we did. We do not tell the person who shared the image who made the request, unless the law requires it.

We can remove images only from Together. We cannot remove copies that someone saved or shared outside the app; section 5 lists services that can help with those.

We use the details in your request only to handle it, as our Privacy Policy describes. Please send a request only in good faith.

4. Report other abuse

  • In the app: report a Moment (open it, open its options, tap Report) or a person ("Report {name}" on their page). A person reviews every report within 24 hours. See Support.
  • Without an account: you can tell us about any illegal content in Together by email; Support explains what to include.
  • Disconnect: "Disconnect {name}" stops someone from sharing with you or contacting you in Together at once.
  • Complaints and appeals: to complain about content, conduct or how we handled your request, or to appeal a decision about your content or account, see Complaints and appeals.

5. More help

  • Images of someone under 18: the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's Take It Down service, takeitdown.ncmec.org.
  • Images of adults: StopNCII.org, stopncii.org, which helps stop images being shared on participating platforms.
