Together is a private app for you and the few people you choose. This policy explains which personal data we process, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what your rights are.

1. The short version

  • There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Together contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software.
  • Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone.
  • When you sign in, what you share with a person is stored so that it can reach them, and only them.
  • Your mood never leaves your iPhone. Neither does your Apple Health data, with one exception: in a shared step goal, your daily step total is shared, and only if you explicitly consent to sharing your progress with the group.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

4. Who sees what you share

  • A Moment, check-in or pick-up is visible only to you and the person it is with. Only you and the recipient can open a Moment's photo or video.
  • Your portrait is visible only to you and the people you are connected with.
  • In a shared goal, the people who join see each other's names, and they can include people you are not connected with. They see your progress only while you share it: each day's value, never your time zone or when it was sent. People who are invited but have not joined, or who declined, see no one's progress. The goal's creator sees who was invited and how each person answered.
  • Widgets: in Settings → Moments on your widgets, you choose whether they show the picture, only who sent what, or nothing. The Lock Screen never shows the picture.
  • Notifications: Moment notifications carry no picture of their own; your iPhone fetches it, following your widget setting. Care notifications show the sender's name and message unless you turn that off in Care. Pick-up notifications never include places or coordinates. A request to connect shows the name of the person asking.
  • If either of you disconnects, sharing between you stops at once, and the other person is told that the connection ended.

5. Step totals from Apple Health: explicit consent

We treat the daily step totals you share in a shared goal as health data and share them only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)):

  • Sharing is off until you switch on "Share my progress with the group" and confirm, by tapping "I consent": "I explicitly consent to sharing my daily step total from Apple Health with this group; I can turn it off at any time."
  • We record when you switched sharing on or off, and the version of the wording you saw.
  • To withdraw: switch "Share my progress with the group" off. The step totals you shared are deleted, and the others stop seeing your progress. You can also remove Together's access to Apple Health in iOS Settings. Withdrawing does not affect sharing that happened before.
  • Nothing else from Apple Health is uploaded. Apple Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

6. Safety features

  • Reports. When you report a Moment or a person, we store the report (who filed it, what or who it concerns, the reason and the time). A person reviews it within 24 hours. The reported person is not told who reported them. Our alert service tells us that a new report exists. It receives a report number, the reason category and the time, never a photo, a video, a caption, a name or a message.
  • Moderation. We may remove a Moment for everyone, end a connection, or ban an account. To decide, we look only at what we need: the reported Moment, or the details of the connection.
  • Sensitive Content Warning (when available). Where your iPhone and Together's version support it, and you have turned on Apple's Sensitive Content Warning (or Communication Safety is on for your account), Together uses Apple's on-device analysis to blur received photos and videos that may contain nudity. Flagged media is not shown on widgets. The analysis runs on your iPhone; neither we nor Apple receive the photo or the result.
  • Word filter. Together will not send a caption, Care message, reply, About text, or goal name or unit that contains a slur, a sexual term or a threat from its list. The check runs on your iPhone. Refused text is not sent to us; your draft stays on your iPhone so you can change it.

10. Deleting your account

In the app, open You → Privacy & account → Delete account and confirm with Apple. You are signed out right away. Deleting your account usually takes a few minutes; if anything delays it, we finish within 30 days. The app confirms when deletion is complete (while the screen is open, or the next time you open it).

We then delete your check-ins, pick-ups, goals, Focus sessions, proofs, devices and settings, your profile and portrait photo, the Moments you sent and their files, your place and progress in shared goals (the shared goals you created are deleted for everyone) and the reports you filed. We revoke your Sign in with Apple token and delete your sign-in.

What remains: so that conversations stay readable for the other person, a "Deleted account" placeholder remains, along with their own reactions and replies. Security records stay without your identity until their 12 months run out. Deleted data in backups expires as section 9 describes. On your iPhone, deleting your account signs you out and removes downloaded Moments; data that lives only on your iPhone, such as your mood, stays until you delete the app.

13. Children

Together is not for children under 16. We do not knowingly let anyone under 16 create an account. If we learn that one has, we delete the account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child uses Together, contact us. Some countries add their own age rules; section 18 explains them (for example the United States, Thailand and Oman).

14. No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you that are based solely on automated processing and that have legal or similarly significant effects. The word filter only stops a single piece of text from being sent, and the Sensitive Content blur runs on your iPhone. A person reviews every report and makes every moderation decision.

15. Do Not Track, cookies and our website

Together does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and our website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Because there is no tracking to switch off, we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals from your browser or device.

16. How we protect your data

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Files are stored in private storage that only the sender and recipient can open. Database access is restricted row by row. On your iPhone, sign-in credentials are kept in the Keychain and app files are protected by iOS data protection. Photos are stripped of location and camera details before they are sent. Server logs record categories of events, not your content. Access to the production systems is limited to the owner, Vlăduţ Stegaru.

17. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top, and change the in-app version to match. If we start to use your data in a significantly new way, we will tell you in the app first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent.
