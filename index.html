Together

A private iPhone app for you and the few people you choose. No ads, no tracking, no public feed.

  • Moments: send a photo, video or GIF to one person you are connected with.
  • Care: a short check-in question, a reply, and an optional acknowledgement.
  • Pick-up: plan a pick-up and, only when the person collecting agrees, see how far away they are.
  • Goals and shared goals: keep personal goals, and join goals with a group if you want to.
  • Widgets: see what your people shared on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

You connect only with someone who accepts your invitation, or whose invitation you accept. Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone. Together needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
